03 — Case study

An 84% faster refresh

The PS1 dashboard at IPD Hinjewadi was slow enough that people stopped trusting it — a 40-minute scheduled refresh meant the numbers on screen were often already out of date by the time anyone opened the report.

The fix wasn't one big change. It was rewriting Power Query steps to fold back to the source instead of processing row-by-row in memory, trimming unused columns and queries early, and rebuilding a handful of DAX measures that were recalculating more context than they needed.

The result: a 6.5-minute refresh, and a dashboard people could rely on again.

After6.5 min

04 — RLS & governance

Who sees what

Every dashboard I deploy ships with access control designed to match how the organization actually works — plant, department, and designation.

Row-Level Security

Dashboards don't show everyone the same thing. RLS scopes data to the viewer based on their role, plant, and designation — so a shop-floor supervisor sees their line, a plant head sees the plant, and leadership sees the full picture.

  • Role definitions mapped to organizational hierarchy — operator, supervisor, plant head, leadership
  • Dynamic RLS using USERPRINCIPALNAME() to filter by signed-in user
  • Static roles for fixed-scope views across plants and departments
  • Tested against every role before deployment, including edge cases at plant and department boundaries
  • Sensitive figures — cost, downtime, breakdown, personnel — masked or hidden by designation

SharePoint permission architecture

Behind every dashboard is a data layer, and that layer needs the same discipline. I design SharePoint permission structures so data access matches dashboard access — no leakage between plants, no over-permissioned accounts.

  • Custom permission levels built around actual job responsibilities rather than defaults
  • SharePoint groups organized by plant, department, and access tier
  • Database access granted through group membership — never per-user
  • Separate read, contribute, and design rights across production and staging lists
  • Generic IDs provisioned for dynamic entry across PS1 and PS2 at IPD Hinjewadi
  • Periodic access reviews to remove stale permissions
